# The Pharhad Road - Publishing And Marketing Writeup

Last reviewed: May 26, 2026
Studio: Teymurian Studios
Engine: Godot 4.6
Use: Steam page, itch.io page, website copy, trailer planning, screenshots, creator pitches, flyers, and playtest notes.

## Positioning

The Pharhad Road is a Persian-inspired turn-based tactical RPG where enemies wake when they see you. You lead pahlavans across one dangerous connected road, manage scarce supplies, and try to keep one careful skirmish from becoming a battlefield-wide disaster.

The game now leans into an original Persian and Shahnameh-inspired fantasy voice:

- Pahlavans hold the line, scout the road, wield Atash, and keep allies alive.
- Divs are the pressure on the road: scouts, sardars, sepahdars, nim-divs, and mountain divs.
- Pharhad can be called as a higher force over the battlefield.
- Dastur Z enters alone with Mehr and Rasti, converting enemies instead of killing them.
- Pahlavan Chess turns the combat system into a fast tactical practice board.

Best hook:

> Enemies wake when they see you. One careful step can control the fight. One bad step can wake the road.

Do not market the game as a D&D product, Forgotten Realms product, Phandalin adventure, official tabletop adaptation, or official SRD module. The safer pitch is original tactical fantasy with dice-driven combat, line-of-sight activation, and one connected road.

## Steam Copy

### Short Description

A Persian-inspired tactical RPG where enemies wake when they see you. Lead four pahlavans across one connected road, manage scarce recovery, and try Command Divs, Call Pharhad, Send Dastur Z, or Pahlavan Chess for alternate tactical chaos.

### About This Game

The Pharhad Road is a compact turn-based tactical RPG about surviving one dangerous route instead of clearing disconnected arenas.

Enemies wake when they see you. A careful move can isolate one fight. A careless move can pull half the road into the same battle.

Lead four fixed-role pahlavans, each with a clear battlefield job:

- Pahlavan: a durable front-line anchor with Gorz Strike, Kaman Bolt, Pahlavan Resolve, and War Drum.
- Rahnavard: a scout and ranged controller with Shamshir Flash, Road Bow, Hunt Sigil, and Simurgh Grace.
- Atash Dastur: a fragile arcane striker with Atash Touch, Atash Bolt, Atash Farr, and Seeking Atash.
- Pari: fast flying support with Pari Kicks, Neyzeh, War Drum, and Simurgh Grace.

Every resource matters. Naan refreshes key powers, Caravanserai restores the party, Pharhad Shrine revives fallen pahlavans, Nush-Daru can save bad turns, and dropped weapons can change a run.

### More Ways To Play

- Lead Pahlavans: lead the party across the road.
- Command Divs: take over active divs and turn the road against the pahlavans.
- Call Pharhad: intervene from above when the battle needs a miracle.
- Send Dastur Z: enter alone with Mehr and Rasti, converting enemies into glowing green allies.
- Pahlavan Chess: fight a quicker tactical training match against rival pahlavans and divs.
- 11s mode: add a real-time pressure clock to player turns.

### Feature Bullets

- Grid-based turn tactics with readable dice rolls.
- Line-of-sight enemy activation that rewards careful scouting.
- Four asymmetric pahlavans with melee, ranged, Atash, healing, movement, and tempo tools.
- Div roster with Div Scouts, Div Sardars, Div Sepahdars, Nim-Divs, and Mountain Divs.
- Scarce recovery through Naan, Nush-Daru, Caravanserai, and Pharhad Shrine.
- Downed-state Bakht trials and clutch stabilizing decisions.
- Autosaves with a Game Over option to restart from the last save.
- Alternate modes for playing pahlavans, active divs, Pharhad, Dastur Z, or Pahlavan Chess.

### Suggested Steam Tags

Turn-Based Tactics, Tactical RPG, Turn-Based Combat, Party-Based RPG, Strategy RPG, Grid-Based Movement, Fantasy, Singleplayer, Tactical, Strategy, RPG, Dice, Pixel Graphics, 2D, Indie, Turn-Based Strategy.

## Trailer Direction

The first 10 seconds should show actual gameplay clearly. Open with a move that wakes enemies.

Recommended 45-60 second structure:

1. Show the road and a careful pahlavan move.
2. Show enemies waking through line of sight.
3. Show Pahlavan, Rahnavard, Atash Dastur, and Pari acting in fast cuts.
4. Show a bad pull where too many enemies wake up.
5. Show items: Nush-Daru, Neyzeh, Naan, Caravanserai, and Pharhad Shrine.
6. Show Command Divs with active divs taking turns.
7. Show Call Pharhad intervention.
8. Show Send Dastur Z converting enemies with Mehr and Rasti.
9. Show Pahlavan Chess with blue player markers and red enemy markers.
10. End on the title and hook: "Control the road, or let the road control the fight."

## Current Strengths To Lead With

- Line-of-sight activation is the mechanical core. The alert radius plus periodic LOS checks creates the tension of "how close can I get?" without feeling arbitrary.
- Resource scarcity feels real. Naan restoring only 1 Resolve and 1 focus, with Caravanserai as a full restore tied to the map, creates meaningful between-fight decisions.
- Four roads plus small level scaling is easy to understand and easy to sell. The player feels the road getting harder without needing a complicated explanation.
- Bakht trials and the 3-success/3-fail stabilization system are tense. A natural 20 revive is a memorable, shareable moment.
- Dastur Z is the strongest alternate-mode concept. A pacifist who can win by converting enemies gives the game a hook most tactics games do not have.
- The Combat Codex is a strong community resource. Link it from the website, itch.io, Steam announcements, and guide posts.
- The creator statement is authentic. "The game I always wanted to play but nobody ever made" should stay visible on the website and in press/creator outreach.

## Launch Gaps To Fix

- Put "Enemies wake when they see you" first wherever possible: Steam short description, itch.io top paragraph, website hero, flyer, and trailer opener.
- Keep the Persian/Shahnameh framing in sentence one. Names like Pahlavan, Pharhad, Div, and Bakht are memorable and should not be hidden.
- Avoid "Command four distinct pahlavans" when describing the main party. Use "Lead four fixed-role pahlavans" so players do not expect deep character customization.
- Add a 15-second GIF or short clip to itch.io showing the enemy wake-up moment. This is the fastest way to teach the hook.
- Remove "Difficult" from primary Steam tags unless the intended audience is challenge-first. Lead with Turn-Based Combat and Party-Based RPG instead.
- Give Pahlavan Chess one standalone screenshot and one sentence in store copy. It can attract tactics puzzle players who may not click for the main campaign alone.
- Verify the flyer PDF on a non-Windows machine before printing or sharing because the generator currently uses Windows system fonts.

## Game Improvement Backlog

- Add a danger preview for movement: "This move may wake 2 enemies" when the destination enters enemy sight.
- Add a small "visible to enemy" tile hint during movement planning, especially for tutorial mode.
- Add one fast Pahlavan Chess screenshot-worthy setup: symmetric teams, clear blue/red frames, and immediate action on turn one.
- Give Dastur Z conversions a short story bark or log line so the pacifist mode feels like the game's spiritual thesis, not only a mechanic.
- Add a "Watch enemy turn / Skip to your turn" option for players who want either drama or speed.
- Keep Road 4 boss difficulty under observation. If first-time players hit a wall, reduce the final boss HP slightly or add a stronger pre-boss recovery opportunity.
- Add a tiny shrine revive story beat that hints at the secret truth without explaining it: the party survives because they are connected by one spirit.
- Make the first-time main menu simpler if playtesters hesitate: one obvious "Begin the Road" path, with alternate modes grouped under "Other Modes."
- Create a short "How to play in 30 seconds" clip for the website and Steam community posts.

## Solo Dev Strengths Worth Mentioning

- OutputText.gd centralizes strings and log rewrites, which makes future translation and wording cleanup much easier.
- The story flag system keeps narrative beats simple and avoids save-system complexity.
- Fog-of-war signature diffing shows real performance care for a grid game on web and mobile.
- Manual d20 mode is charming and unusual. It lets players use physical dice without requiring special hardware.
- Six play modes at launch give the game more replay depth than its size suggests.
- The Python flyer generator makes store and print marketing reproducible instead of hand-built once.
