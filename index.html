Built for tactical choices

The Pharhad Road mixes readable dice with clean digital tactics: line-of-sight activation, movement ranges, action economy, ranged attacks, healing, enemy conversion, autosave restarts, and recovery decisions between fights.

Four hero roles

Command Vanguard, Wayfinder, Ember Sage, and Becky. Each hero brings a different tactical job to the road.

Wake enemies carefully

Enemies join the fight when sight lines expose the party. One careful move can isolate a threat. One bad move can wake the road.

Dice-driven danger

Critical hits, misses, saves, downed heroes, recovery tools, and scarce supplies create tense survival choices.

Multiple modes

Play Heroes, Play Monsters, Play Pharhad, Send Cleric Z, Hero Chess, or turn on 11s mode for pressure turns.

Connected battlefield

Move through a continuous route instead of isolated rooms. The road itself becomes the encounter.

Compact run length

Built for focused tactical sessions: a first run can be longer, while replay runs and Hero Chess are faster.

“Control the road, or let the road control the fight.”

Quick pitch

The Pharhad Road is a compact fantasy tactics game from Teymurian Studios for players who enjoy grid movement, party roles, tactical positioning, and dice-based risk without needing a giant RPG campaign.

  • Turn-based grid combat
  • Line-of-sight enemy activation
  • Small-party RPG combat
  • Resource pressure across a connected map
  • Built in Godot
